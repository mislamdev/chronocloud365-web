This policy explains how ChronoCloud365 Technologies Pvt. Ltd. (“we”, “us”) handles personal data. It covers this website, our marketing communications, and the services we deliver to clients. Where we act only as a processor on behalf of a client, that client’s privacy notice takes precedence and we process data on their documented instructions.

1. Who we are

ChronoCloud365 Technologies Pvt. Ltd. is a company registered in Bangladesh, with its registered office at Mirpur, Dhaka-1216. For data protection purposes we act as the data controller for information you give us directly, and as a data processor for information processed inside our client platforms.

2. What we collect

Information you give us

  • Contact details: name, company, work email address, phone number and job role.
  • Enquiry content: the messages, briefs and documents you send us.
  • Recruitment information: CVs, cover letters and interview notes.
  • Billing and banking details, processed by our payment providers rather than stored by us in full.

Information collected automatically

  • Device and browser information, IP address and approximate location derived from it.
  • Pages visited, referring pages and interaction data on this website.
  • Diagnostic logs from the client platform when you ask us to investigate a support issue.

Information we do not collect

We do not knowingly collect special category data (such as health, biometric or political information) through this website. If a client uses our platform for such purposes, it is their responsibility to configure it appropriately and notify us through the appropriate contractual channel.

3. How we use it

  • To respond to enquiries and to prepare scoping, proposals and demonstrations.
  • To deliver, support, secure and improve the products and services you have bought.
  • To send service notices, release notes and product updates you have asked for.
  • To maintain the security, availability and performance of our systems.
  • To meet legal, tax and accounting obligations.
  • To improve our products through aggregated, de-identified usage analysis.

We do not sell personal data, and we do not share it for third-party advertising.

4. Legal bases

Where the GDPR or the DPDP Act applies, we rely on: performance of a contract for data needed to deliver services you have bought; legitimate interests for operating and securing our business; consent for marketing communications and for any non-essential cookies; and compliance with legal obligation for statutory records.

5. Sharing

We share personal data only with: subprocessors who host, secure or support our services, under written contracts and only as instructed; professional advisers, auditors and regulators where we are legally required to; and a successor entity in the event of a merger or sale, in which case we will tell you before your data moves. We do not sell personal data to third parties.

6. Retention

Enquiry records are kept for 24 months. Recruitment records for 12 months, or longer with your consent. Client records for the duration of the contract and 7 years afterwards to meet statutory accounting requirements. Infrastructure logs for 12 months. You can ask us to delete anything earlier, subject to our legal obligations.

7. Security

Data is encrypted in transit with TLS 1.2 or above and at rest with AES-256. Access is role-based, reviewed quarterly and logged. Multi-factor authentication is enforced for all staff with platform or data access. Our platform is tested annually by independent penetration testers, and our controls are documented against ISO 27001 and SOC 2 frameworks.

8. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access your personal data, correct it, delete it, restrict or object to its processing, receive it in a portable format, withdraw consent, and complain to a supervisory authority. We respond to verified requests within 30 days and never charge for them.

9. Cookies

This website uses strictly necessary cookies to operate core functions, including remembering your colour-scheme preference. Analytics cookies are set only with consent, and you can change your choice at any time from your browser or by clearing site data. We do not use advertising cookies.

10. Contact

Questions, requests or complaints about privacy should go to our Data Protection Officer at privacy@chronocloud365.com, or by post to the registered office address above. If you are unsatisfied with our response, you may complain to the relevant supervisory authority — in Bangladesh, the Data Protection Commissioner.

We review this policy at least annually and whenever our processing changes materially. Material changes are notified to clients and website users by email.
