Flagship · Retail & point of sale

Cart POS

Retailers do not lose sales at the till. They lose them in the gap between the website cart, the mobile app and the counter — where the basket, the stock and the price are three different answers to the same question.

Cart POS is one basket that follows the customer through every channel. Add online, pay at the counter, return it in store. It works when the network does not, and it reconciles before the shutters come down.

  • Offline-first checkout. Transactions queue locally and sync when the connection returns, so a dead link never stops a queue.
  • One catalogue. Variants, price lists, promotions and stock come from MagicERP, or straight from Cart POS if you do not run the ERP.
  • Same-day close. Cash count, drawer variance, card settlement and VAT summary reconciled at end of day, not end of month.
