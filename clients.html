Case study 01

Clyfar Fashion

Fashion retail · 24 stores across 4 cities · 340 staff

MagicERP Multi-Store Retail POS ChronoFlow ChronoReach ChronoCloud

The challenge

Stock was counted on spreadsheets. Four stores and a warehouse each kept their own version, so buyers over-ordered while flagship shelves sat empty — and nobody could prove which number was right. Seasonal collections took three weeks to reconcile after launch.

What we did

A single MagicERP instance covering every store, warehouse and head office, with a unified catalogue and size/colour variant logic. Counter POS replaced three separate systems, ChronoFlow auto-raised purchase orders against live stock, and ChronoReach took over campaigns that previously lived in a shared inbox.

Case study 02

Elegant Electric

Electrical engineering & EPC · B2B & infrastructure · 180 staff

MagicERP Project Costing Field Service ChronoSecure ChronoDesk

The challenge

Twelve years of project history lived in one accountant's spreadsheet. Quoting a new installation meant guessing at material and labour rates from memory, so profitable work was being priced wrong and nobody could see margin until it was too late to change it.

What we did

We rebuilt project costing inside MagicERP: a rate card that updates with supplier prices, live margin on every estimate, and a field service module that schedules engineers and captures site photos against the job. Twelve years of history were migrated, then the estate was hardened and moved under ChronoSecure and ChronoDesk.

Case study 03

City Stanja

Fashion & design label · Direct-to-consumer · 45 staff

ChronoReach Custom Storefront ChronoFlow MagicERP ChronoInsight

The challenge

A strong design label with no route to market online. The studio produced two collections a year; every launch required a manual spreadsheet handoff to fulfilment, and by the time an order arrived the stock picture was already out of date.

What we did

We built a storefront that reads directly from MagicERP, so stock, sizes and pricing are always the truth. ChronoFlow handles the launch sequence — photography, product load, campaign and order routing. ChronoReach runs the lifecycle campaigns and the whole loop reports into ChronoInsight.
