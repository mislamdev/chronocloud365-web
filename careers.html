Hiring

What to expect.

Four conversations and a practical exercise. We tell you the compensation band before you spend an afternoon on interviews.

  1. Step 01

    Intro call

    30 minutes with the hiring manager. What the role is, what it pays, what the team looks like.

  2. Step 02

    Technical round

    A conversation about how you think, plus a discussion of a real system we built.

  3. Step 03

    Practical exercise

    A scoped, take-home task — paid if it takes more than four hours. Never used as free work.

  4. Step 04

    Meet the team

    You interview the people you would work with, and they interview you. Offer within 5 working days.
