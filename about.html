Our story

It started with one stubborn retailer.

In 2014 our founders were running implementation projects for a mid-sized fashion retailer that had eleven stores, four spreadsheets and a stock count that changed depending on who you asked. The owner did not need a bigger ERP. She needed the numbers to be the same in every building, every day.

We built it. Then we noticed the same pattern everywhere we looked — a company that had outgrown its software, a finance team re-keying data that already existed somewhere else, a marketing team working from a list that had not been updated since Tuesday. The technology was never the hard part. Connecting it was.

ChronoCloud365 exists to remove that gap. We build our own ERP, our own automation platform, our own marketing stack and our own cloud operations, and we make them share one data model. When a client buys one product, the rest is already waiting and connected.

  • We ship software that matches how the business really works, not how a template assumes it works.
  • We price engagements with a written scope, a fixed date and no surprise invoices.
  • We stay after go-live, because the hard problems start after go-live.
  • We build on our own infrastructure, so we are accountable for uptime and security.

What we believe

Four commitments we actually keep.

Clarity over theatre

Written scope, fixed price, real dates. If a phase will slip, you hear it from us before you notice it in production — not in a monthly status deck.

We stay accountable

We run the infrastructure, the patching, the monitoring and the support. When something breaks at 2am, the person who fixes it is the team that built it.

The user's day matters

A cashier scanning a queue, a warehouse hand without gloves. We design for the person using the screen, not for the diagram in the slide deck.

No lock-in, no hostage data

Full exports, documented APIs and an open data model on every plan. Your data is yours, and leaving should be easy if that is ever your decision.
